MOVEMENT

WASD / ↑↓←→
Move in any cardinal direction. Hold two keys at once for diagonal movement.
🕹️ Joystick
Full analog stick on mobile. Push any direction including diagonals. Speed scales with how far you push the stick.

COMBAT — 🔥 FIRE BUTTON (F / Space)

F / Space / 🔥
Weapons only. Melee and AoE weapons fire instantly in the direction you last moved. Ranged weapons (bow, crossbow) fire in your facing direction. Mines are placed at your feet. This button never defuses or hides mines.
Click canvas
PC only: click anywhere on the canvas to aim a ranged weapon toward that point and fire immediately.
1 – 5
Equip a hotbar weapon by slot number. Slots fill in pickup order; overflow lives in the 🎒 backpack (press I). On mobile, tap any weapon slot in the HUD.

INTERACT — 🔧 INTERACT BUTTON (E)

E / 🔧
Defuse: stand adjacent to an enemy mine and hold — a purple countdown appears. Hold still for 2 seconds to defuse it. Moving or releasing cancels it.

Camouflage: after placing one of your own mines, stand on or adjacent to it and hold — a green countdown appears. Hold for 2 seconds to hide it from the Minotaur. Releasing resets the timer; press again to continue. Walk away and the mode cancels.

OTHER

ESC
Cancel aim mode, abort a defuse, or cancel camouflage.

CAMOUFLAGING YOUR MINES 🫥

1
Place a mine with the 🔥 fire button. A toast says "PRESS E/🔧 TO HIDE".
2
Stand on or adjacent to the mine. Press and hold 🔧 (E key). A green countdown fills around the mine.
3
Hold for the full 2 seconds without moving. Releasing resets the timer — press again to restart. Walk away and the mode cancels entirely.
4
Once camouflaged the mine appears as a faint green dot. The Minotaur treats it as open floor and will walk over it on the optimal path.
💡
A Minotaur that sees a visible mine on its path stops to defuse it — leaving it standing still and open to a stun (the Scout and Tactician defuse fast, the Blind One is slow). Use hidden mines to ambush it outright on its favourite route to the exit.

ENEMY MINES ⚠

💣
The Minotaur picks up mines and lays them strategically on your path to the exit. Stepping on one kills you instantly. Stand adjacent and hold 🔧 (E key) for 2 seconds to defuse. The Sixth Sense shop item makes enemy mines glow through the fog.

👻 GHOST REPLAY

👻
During a run: after your first escape the status bar shows a 👻 split against your best clear time for the current maze size — green means you're ahead of your ghost.
▶️
At the Merchant: tick 👻 WATCH REPLAY (top of the shop) to rewatch your last escape on its own maze, with a faint ghost dot running your best pace beside you. The split tells you, second by second, whether you beat your old self.

UNLOCKABLE MODES

⚔ DAILY
Daily Gauntlet — one attempt per day on a maze identical for every player. Tier III, a loaner club + bow, no perks, no progression at stake. The attempt is spent the moment you enter. Copy your result and compare.
⚔ IRONMAN
One continuous life — keep escaping maze after maze with your full build, the difficulty climbing, until your first death ends everything. No resume, no retry. Posts to its own leaderboard. Unlocks at 10,000 high score.
🐂 HUNTER
You ARE the Minotaur. Run the prey down before it reaches the exit. Pick a class — each unlocks on a high-score ladder: 🐂 Minotaur 5k · 🦅 Scout 10k · 🦬 Bruiser 15k · 💣 Tactician 20k · 🙈 Blind One 25k. The prey gets smarter the higher your best score climbs. Its own leaderboard.

SCORING & SAVING

ESCAPE +150
Score for escaping. Grows by +25 for each run you have previously won — so run 5 pays 250, run 10 pays 375, and so on. Score is prestige: it feeds the leaderboard and your best.
DEATH −50
Score penalty for dying. Score cannot go below 0. Death does not advance your run count or difficulty tier.
💰 GOLD
The currency. Found in piles on the floor (richest inside the treasure vault) and earned by selling stashed weapons to the Merchant. Gold buys shop items; score does not.
💾 Auto-Save
Your run autosaves as you move, and your progress saves on every escape — score, run count, tier, permanent upgrades, stash and best score. Close the app any time; CONTINUE RUN picks up where you stood.

DIFFICULTY TIERS — EVERY 5 WINS

Every 5 wins the Minotaur gets faster and reacts more quickly. Every 10 wins an additional Minotaur joins the maze.

TIER I
1 Minotaur · Slow deliberate movement · Long reaction window
TIER II
1 Minotaur · Noticeably faster · Quicker path recalculation
TIER III
2 Minotaurs · Approaches original speed · Coordinated hunting from two directions
VARIANTS
🐂 Classic — armed with a club, predicts your path.  🦬 Bruiser — slow, no weapons, breaks walls.  🦅 Scout — fast, ranged only, half damage.  🙈 Blind One — hears movement anywhere on the map; standing still loses it completely.  💣 Tactician — looks like the Classic but ignores you — it's busy laying mines on your path to the exit every 10 seconds. Composition is random each run.
TIER IV
3 Minotaurs · Fast · Aggressive · Short shoot cooldown
TIER V
4 Minotaurs · Maximum speed · Near-constant pressure from multiple angles
ASCENSION
Beyond 25 wins every run carries a rotating rite — 🕯️ fading light, 🫥 veiled mines, 👥 twin wardens, ⚡ swift death, or a 🔒 sealed exit. The status bar shows the active rite's mark.

THE MAZE

🏛️
The maze grows larger every 5 wins. The camera follows you — the exit is always on the right side. The maze has multiple routes so a mined path can often be circumvented.
💡
Tip: If the status bar says PATH MINED, the Minotaur has blocked your direct route. Find an alternate corridor or use 🔧 to defuse the mine.
💡
Tip: Ranged weapons fire in the direction you last moved. Face the Minotaur by walking toward it one step, then fire without moving.
💡
Tip: Shadow (👁️) completely stops the Minotaur from targeting you, shooting, or catching you for 7 seconds. Great for sprinting to the exit.
💡
Tip: The screen shakes and glitches more intensely as the Minotaur closes in. Trust the rumble — if it's bad, it's close even through walls.

THE NEW RITES

🔒
Sealed doors: some runs lock the exit behind a 🗝️ key. The key spawns far from your path — sometimes inside the treasure vault. The status bar reads FIND THE KEY until you hold it.
🧠
The Warden remembers: the labyrinth studies how you play and sends hunters chosen to counter it. Trappers draw Tacticians, sprinters draw Scouts and the Blind One, and standing statues draw converging packs. Change your habits to change the hunt.
📜
Modes & extras: the Daily Gauntlet, Ironman, Hunter Mode, Merchant Contracts and the Ghost Replay all live in the MODES tab.
